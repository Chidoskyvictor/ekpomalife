🎱 8 Ball Pool

Controls

  • Aim: drag on the table. ◀ ▶ fine-tune.
  • Shoot: drag the power bar right, let go.
  • Spin: drag the blue dot on the white ball. Top = follow, bottom = screw back, sides = side spin.
  • Ball in hand: drag the white anywhere (on the break: behind the line).

The game

  • The table is open after the break. The first ball you pot gives you solids (1–7) or stripes (9–15).
  • Always hit one of your own balls first. Pot one of yours and you shoot again.
  • Clear your group, then call a pocket and pot the 8 there to win.

Fouls → ball in hand for the other player

  • Hitting the wrong ball first, or no ball at all.
  • Potting the white.
  • After contact, no ball reaches a cushion and nothing is potted.
  • Online, each shot has a 30-second clock.

You lose if

  • You pot the 8 before your group is cleared, pot it in the wrong pocket, or foul while potting it.
