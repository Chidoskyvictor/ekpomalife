Keep this file. It is our proof of permission for every car model in the game and trailers.

## 2012 Lexus RX 350 → `public/models/cars/lexus-rx.glb`
- Source: Sketchfab, "2012 Lexus RX 350" (free download)
- Licence: **Sketchfab Free Standard** — "use worldwide, on all types of media, commercially or not, and in all types of derivative works" (screenshot shown by the owner on 2026-11-21)
- Our changes: Lexus "L" badges removed (front grille, tailgate), resized to real size (4.89 m), wheels split into Wheel_FL/FR/RL/RR, stray spare wheel removed, simplified to ~103k triangles, textures 512 px, meshopt-compressed (24 MB → 1.95 MB). Work file: `brand/cars/lexus-rx350-work.blend`.
- Rules: no Lexus logo/name in the game; never offer the raw model as a download.
